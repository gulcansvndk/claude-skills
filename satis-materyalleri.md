## 1. 30 saniyelik kapı konuşması (door pitch)

**Ne zaman:** Barın sakin saatleri, yani açılıştan önce (genelde 15:00–17:00). Bar müdürünü ya da baş barmeni isteyin. Yanınızda 2–3 örnek poşet ve tek sayfalık ürün föyü olsun.

> Hi! I'm Gülcan, co-founder of **[Brand Name]**. We make dehydrated citrus garnishes right here in San Diego: lemon, lime, orange, blood orange and grapefruit wheels.
>
> My husband Muammer is a working bar manager and mixologist, so every wheel is cut the way bartenders actually want it: even thickness, crisp, bright color, and it floats.
>
> Dehydrated wheels mean no daily cutting, no waste at the end of the night, and the same perfect garnish on every drink.
>
> Can I leave you a free sample pack to try this week? I'll stop back on **[day]** to hear what your team thinks.

**İtiraz gelirse kısa cevaplar:**

| They say… | You say… |
|---|---|
| "We cut fresh fruit." | "Totally, and many bars do both. Dehydrated wheels are great for high-volume nights, rimless drinks, and anything stirred where you want a clean look with zero prep." |
| "We already buy from someone." | "Great, then you know the category. Try ours side by side. We're local, made in small batches, and I deliver myself." |
| "How much?" | "It depends on the pack size. Here's our sheet. Most bars start with a 100-wheel pack." |
| "Not now." | "No problem. Can I leave the sample and my card? I'll check back in a couple of weeks." |
